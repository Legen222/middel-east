## 1 · What this is

SCRAPLINE (working name) is a play-money demo of a gambling site set in a post-apocalyptic world built from scrap: crates, raids, scrapyards, workbenches. Players are 18+. The site is dark graphite with cyan, violet and brass accents. Most pictures are still thin line drawings; the images below replace them.

Screenshots of the current site (`platform/web/art/reference/reference-*.png`) show the colours and the mood to match. The thin line drawings in them are what your images replace.

| Who | Does what |
|---|---|
| **You (ChatGPT)** | Draw the images, one per brief, and keep the style consistent across all of them. |
| **Claude** (the developer) | Wrote these briefs. Reviews every image, cuts and compresses it and builds it into the site. |
| **The owner** | Approves the style test and passes files between you and Claude. |

## 2 · How to work

1. **Style test first.** Draw the 4 images of batch `style-test` and show them to the owner. Go on only once the owner approves the look. If they want changes, adjust and redo the style test.
2. **Then batch by batch**, in this order: `crates`, `tiles`, `banners`, `avatars`, `illustrations`.
3. **The prompt for each image** is the style (§ 3) + the rules of its kind (§ 5) + its motif (§ 6). Every image of one kind matches the approved style test: same camera angle, same light, same materials.
4. **One image per asset.** Check it against the rules (§ 4) before handing it over; redo it if it breaks one.
5. **Canvas:** square 1024 × 1024, portrait 1024 × 1536 or landscape 1536 × 1024, as its kind says.
6. **Transparent background** where the kind says so: a real transparent PNG (alpha channel). Not white, not black and not a checkerboard pattern painted into the picture.

**Delivery**
- File name: exactly the asset id plus `.png`, for example `crate-barrel.png`.
- If you can write to the repository `legen222/middel-east`: put the files in `platform/web/art/incoming/` on a new branch `art/<batch>` and open a pull request. Change nothing else.
- Otherwise: hand the owner the files with exactly these names, one batch at a time. The owner passes them on to Claude.
- Claude ticks an asset off below once its image is in the site.

## 3 · Style (every image)

Original stylized game art for a dark, premium web app set in a post-apocalyptic survival world built from scrap. Semi-realistic 3D render with clean hard-surface modelling: worn painted metal, rust patina, rivets, bolts, scratched edges. Cool cyan rim light (#3DDCFF) with a subtle violet accent (#9B7BFF), warm brass highlights (#D9B467), deep graphite shadows (#0A0C0F). Crisp, high contrast, readable at small sizes, consistent lighting from the upper left. No text, letters, numbers or symbols with writing, no logos, no watermarks, no UI elements, no people's faces. Not cute, not cartoonish, nothing aimed at children. Not in the style of any specific existing game; no recognizable trademarks, brand designs or existing weapon skins.

**Palette:** graphite #0A0C0F and #111419, cyan #3DDCFF, violet #9B7BFF, brass #D9B467, green #3EE89A, red #FF5C6C (warnings only).

## 4 · Rules (never break them)

- **No writing anywhere:** no text, letters, numbers or signs that look like writing, no watermarks, no signatures.
- **No logos, brands or trademarks:** nothing from Rust, Facepunch, Steam, Valve, any casino or skin-trading site, any weapon maker.
- **Nothing copied:** no icons or item designs from existing games, no Steam workshop skins, no art from other gambling sites. Our own designs only.
- **No item pictures:** the items in the crates (weapons, armour, skins, resources) show their real pictures from Steam. Draw only what is listed below.
- **No Red Cross emblem** (a red cross, especially on white). Medical things are mint green with a white plus.
- **No real people and no visible faces.** Nobody who looks under 25. Nothing cute, childish or cartoonish: gambling images must not appeal to minors.
- **No real money:** no banknotes, coins of real currencies, casino chips, slot machines or piles of cash. The site's currency is Scrap: pieces of scrap metal and brass nuts.
- **No blood, no gore, nobody hurt.** Weapons are objects only, never aimed at the viewer.
- **`ill-break` stays calm:** it goes on the responsible-gambling pages, so no winning, no crates opening, no rewards.

## 5 · Kinds

### crate · square 1024 × 1024 · **transparent background**

- **Used for:** crate cards, the crate-opening reel, Case Battle and the odds tables, shown from about 60 to 300 px.
- **Kind rules:** A single object, centered, three-quarter view from slightly above, filling about 80 % of the frame, soft contact shadow directly under it, nothing else in the scene, transparent background.

### tile · portrait 1024 × 1536 · full background

- **Used for:** game cards in the lobby and the Games menu, about 220 × 300 px, game title laid over the top.
- **Kind rules:** Moody graphite-black backdrop. Portrait game cover illustration. The subject sits in the middle of the frame; the top fifth is calm, dark, low-detail space for a title overlay; nothing important near the edges, because the image is cropped to 3:4 and to 16:10 around its centre. Edge-to-edge scene, cinematic depth of field.

### banner · landscape 1536 × 1024 · full background

- **Used for:** lobby hero, lobby slider and the link preview, wide, a headline laid over the left side.
- **Kind rules:** Moody graphite-black backdrop. Wide cinematic banner. The focal subject sits in the right half; the left 45 % is dark, low-detail negative space for a headline. Edge-to-edge scene.

### avatar · square 1024 × 1024 · full background

- **Used for:** player pictures in chat, battles and the leaderboard, 30 to 64 px, cropped to a circle.
- **Kind rules:** Square profile picture: head and shoulders of a survivor whose face is completely hidden behind a mask, helmet or visor (no skin of the face visible). Plain dark graphite background with one coloured rim light. Centered so it still works cropped to a circle.

### backdrop · landscape 1536 × 1024 · full background

- **Used for:** page background behind everything, dimmed further by the site.
- **Kind rules:** Full-page website background that sits behind text and panels: very dark and low contrast overall, detail only as dim silhouettes in the bottom third, the upper half an almost empty night sky, no bright spot in the middle. Panoramic, so it can be cropped left and right.

### illustration · square 1024 × 1024 · **transparent background**

- **Used for:** empty states and reward cards, about 120 to 200 px.
- **Kind rules:** A small spot illustration for an empty state or a section header: one simple object or a tiny scene, centered, generous empty space around it, transparent background, readable at 160 px.
